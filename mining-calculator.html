Formulas Used

P(success in t seconds) = 1 - e^(-(your_hashrate × t) ÷ expected_hashes) Waiting for inputs.
network_share = your_hashrate ÷ network_hashrate Waiting for inputs.
next_block_odds ≈ network_share Waiting for inputs.
expected_blocks_in_window = t ÷ expected_seconds Waiting for inputs.

Each hash is modeled as an independent random draw from a 256-bit space. Increasing hashrate increases the number of draws per second; it does not make any individual nonce smarter.
